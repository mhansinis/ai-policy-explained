AI Policy, Explained

A monthly digest of AI policy, in plain language.

By Mariyan Silva

The AI policy landscape shifts constantly, and keeping pace with it is part of the job for anyone working in this field. It matters just as much to be able to explain what's happening in plain language that a client or colleague without a legal or technical background can actually follow. That's what this digest is for. It's a single monthly round-up of the developments worth knowing, drawn only from reputable sources, with a Harvard-style reference for every item so you can verify it yourself and dig deeper wherever you want.
