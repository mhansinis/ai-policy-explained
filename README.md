# AI Policy, Explained

A monthly digest of developments in artificial intelligence policy, regulation, and governance, written in plain language for readers without a legal or technical background.

**Live site:** https://mhansinis.github.io/ai-policy-explained/

By Mariyan Silva.

---

## What this is

Each month, this project gathers notable news on AI policy from reputable sources, explains why each development matters in accessible language, and publishes a styled digest. Every item carries a Harvard-style reference so readers can verify it and read further.

Reports are organised by region (EU, US, UK, Asia-Pacific, Global) and then by topic (Regulation & Law, Enforcement, National Strategy, Industry Response, Safety & Standards).

## How it works

The digest is produced by an automated pipeline:

1. **Collection** — recent AI policy news is gathered from reputable outlets via the NewsAPI service.
2. **Screening and explanation** — each article is assessed for genuine policy relevance and, where relevant, summarised in plain language and tagged by region and topic.
3. **Storage** — screened items are stored in a structured database.
4. **Compilation** — each month's items are compiled into a single styled HTML report with inline citations and a full reference list.
5. **Publishing** — the report is published here and linked from the homepage archive.

## Structure

- `index.html` — homepage and archive of all issues
- `july-2026.html` — the July 2026 edition (and one file per month thereafter)

## Sources and referencing

All items are drawn from established news outlets. Each is cited in Harvard style, with clickable inline citations linking to a full reference list at the foot of every issue. The digest summarises and comments on reporting from these sources; it does not reproduce their articles.
